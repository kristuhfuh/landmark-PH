## Design Context

### Users
Guests exploring stays, dining and activities at Landmark Port Harcourt, on phones and desktop screens. Help them discover the grounds and plan a visit or booking.

### Brand Personality
Refined, welcoming, confident. The experience should feel like polished coastal hospitality.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the existing marine, orange and sand brand colours. Use upright typography, fully rounded buttons, generous spacing and clear content hierarchy. Aim for a launch-ready finish while preserving the site's photography and scroll experiences.

### Design Principles
- Make discovery and booking straightforward across screen sizes.
- Use consistent, readable typography and controls.
- Give photography room to breathe; keep decoration restrained.
- Show information guests need, without internal planning labels or area measurements.
- Keep keyboard focus visible and respect reduced motion.
